In the mist-shrouded hills of the countryside, a majestic _shishi_ lay dying. Its tawny coat matted with dirt, its once-proud mane now tangled and dull. The _shishi_ had been hunting with its companion, _tanuki_ Kaito, when it spotted a plump _yakitori_ grazing on the hillside above. The _shishi_ stalked its prey, its eyes fixed on the tender flesh, and with a swift pounce, it brought down the unsuspecting bird.

With its hunger sated, the _shishi_ set off to quench its thirst at the nearby _kawa_. "Kaito, my old friend," it called out, "I'll be back soon. Keep watch over my _yakitori_ while I'm away." Kaito, ever the dutiful companion, nodded and replied, "Yes, _shishi_-san. I'll guard it well."

As the _shishi_ disappeared into the trees, Kaito's eyes grew hungry. He glanced at his wife, O-ka, who was busy tidying up their _hut_. With a subtle nod, he whispered, "O-ka, give me the _mori_ rope, the one that's been hanging by the door." O-ka, understanding the unspoken request, handed him a thin, gnarled cord. "Here, Kaito-san," she said, her voice barely audible.

As Kaito pretended to lower the rope for the _shishi_ to climb up, he gave it a sly tug. The rope snapped, and the _shishi_ tumbled down the hillside, its paws scrabbling at the air. Kaito beat a hollow _taiko_ drum that lay nearby, shouting, "O-ka, why did you give me such a rotten rope? I told you to get the sturdy one from the _yakitori_ farm!"

The _shishi_ , enraged and wounded, roared at Kaito to stop his drumming and help it climb up. But Kaito simply smiled and handed down a new rope, this one even thinner and more brittle than the first. As the _shishi_ neared the top, Kaito gave it a sudden yank, and the rope broke once more. The _shishi_ fell again, this time more severely injured.

Kaito strolled over to the _shishi_ , feigning concern. "Oh, _shishi_-san, are you okay? Is it much pain?" The _shishi_ lay there, its eyes dull with pain and defeat. Kaito and O-ka exchanged a knowing glance, and with a slow, deliberate pace, they began to walk away, leaving the wounded _shishi_ to its fate.

As they vanished into the trees, the sound of Kaito's drumming grew fainter, but the _shishi_ 's roar of anger and pain echoed through the hills, a haunting reminder of the treachery that had been perpetrated. The mist closed in, shrouding the scene in a veil of secrecy, and the _shishi_ lay still, its once-majestic form now a mere shadow of its former self.
