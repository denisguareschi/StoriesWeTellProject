In the misty dawn of a bygone era, when the heavens were still young and the gods whispered secrets to mortals, Emperor Kotei, the son of Emperor Yuhi, ruled over our fair land. His mother, the Empress, had foreseen his greatness, for she beheld the North Star, that steadfast sentinel of the heavens, flashing with fiery brilliance as she strolled beneath the starry canopy on a balmy summer evening. And lo, Kotei was born, a child of destiny.

As he grew in wisdom and strength, the Emperor faced a dire challenge from the wicked magician, Shiyu. This sorcerer, with a head forged of iron, had usurped the hearts of many, and his malevolent powers seemed invincible. Kotei, undaunted, rallied his loyal army and marched against the rebel, but Shiyu's cunning and magic allowed him to escape, leaving the Emperor's forces bewildered and lost in the dense fogs that shrouded the battlefield.

Yet Kotei, a master of the human heart and a weaver of wisdom, refused to yield. He pondered deeply, seeking a solution to this riddle of the rebel's escape. And thus, the South Pointing shinansha was born, a wondrous device with a figure pointing ever southward, a compass that would guide the Emperor's troops through the thickest of fogs.

With this marvelous instrument in hand, Kotei led his army into battle once more. The rebel's magic still sought to confound them, but the Emperor's men, steadfast and true, followed the direction of the shinansha, and their march was unerring. They pursued Shiyu's army to the banks of a swollen river, where the magician, with his dark arts, had passed over, seeking refuge in a fortress beyond.

Kotei, wise and just, ordered his men to build boats, that they might cross the river and bring the war to its final end. And lo, the Emperor's ingenuity and perseverance succeeded, for the boats were built, and the army crossed, pursuing the rebel into his stronghold. The battle was fierce, but in the end, Kotei emerged victorious, his people rejoicing at the dawn of peace.

Yet, even as the Emperor's reign reached its hundredth year, a sign from the heavens proclaimed its end. A great Dragon, born of the celestial realms, descended upon the earth, and Kotei, wise and knowing, understood the message. He smiled, for he had lived a life of purpose, and now his spirit was called to ascend to the heavens, there to guide and watch over his people from the stars.

The Empress and the courtiers, awestruck and frightened, beheld the Dragon, and the Emperor's words, "Do not be afraid, for this is a messenger from Heaven," calmed their hearts. They approached the Dragon, but alas, it could not bear the weight of so many. The Empress and a few courtiers rode in triumph, while the others, clinging to the Dragon's beard, fell to the earth, their hearts heavy with sorrow.

As the Dragon vanished into the heavens, a bow and an arrow, tokens of the Emperor's presence, fell to the courtyard, a reminder to his people of the life and legacy of Kotei, the great Emperor, whose spirit continues to guide us to this day.
