My brothers and sisters, gather 'round and listen closely, for I have a tale to tell that will take you on a journey through the ages. It is the story of Kotei, the great Emperor of our ancestors, who ruled with wisdom and justice.

In those ancient days, when the world was still young and mysterious, Kotei's mother had a vision that foretold the birth of a great son. She was a wise and respected woman, known for her deep understanding of the spirits and the workings of the universe. And when her son was born, the stars in the sky shone brightly, as if to celebrate the arrival of this special child.

Kotei grew up to be a just and fair ruler, loved by his people. But he faced many challenges during his reign, none greater than the evil magician Shiyu. This wicked sorcerer had a head made of iron, and his powers of darkness were feared by all. He would often bring down thick fogs to confuse and disorient Kotei's armies, making it impossible for them to fight.

But Kotei was not one to be defeated so easily. He thought deeply and pondered long, seeking a way to outwit the magician and bring peace to his people. And so, he invented the shinansha, a chariot with a figure that always pointed south, guiding him through the thickest of fogs.

With the shinansha, Kotei led his armies into battle once more, determined to vanquish the forces of darkness. And though Shiyu brought down the fog, Kotei's soldiers were not confused, for they followed the guidance of the shinansha. They drove the magician's army back, until they reached the swollen river that blocked their path.

But even the shinansha could not help Kotei cross the river, and so he was forced to wait. And in his waiting, he observed the world around him. He saw a spider trying to cross the pond, using a floating leaf as a bridge. And he thought, "Why not build a boat, just like the spider's leaf, to carry my people across the river?"

And so, Kotei invented the first boat, and with it, his armies were able to cross the river and attack Shiyu's headquarters. The magician was defeated, and peace was restored to the land.

But Kotei's reign was not without its challenges. He reigned for many long years, and in that time, he invented many other things that benefited his people. He was a wise and just ruler, beloved by his subjects.

And when his time on earth had come to an end, a great Dragon came from the sky, to take him home to the heavens. The Dragon was a messenger from the spirits, come to take Kotei back to the realm of the ancestors. And though his people were sad to see him go, they knew that he had done his duty, and that his legacy would live on.

So let us remember the story of Kotei, and the lessons it teaches us. Let us strive to be wise and just, like our great ancestor, and to use our gifts and talents to benefit our people. May his spirit guide us, and may we always remember the importance of following our hearts and our consciences.
