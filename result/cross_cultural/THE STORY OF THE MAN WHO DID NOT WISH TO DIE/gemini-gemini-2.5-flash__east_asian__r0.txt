Mukashi mukashi, lived Fukutomi Sentaro, a man of comfortable means who idled away his youth. At thirty-two winters, the shadow of death fell upon his heart. "I wish to live," he murmured, "for five or six hundred years, free from suffering. A man's ordinary life is but a fleeting dream." He recalled ancient Tennō and the Princess of Yamato, famed for their centuries-long lives. He also knew of Shin no Shikōtei, the powerful Chinese Emperor, who feared death and sent Jofuku-sama to Horaizan, the mythical isle of *sennin* and the Elixir of Immortality. Jofuku-sama never returned but is revered in Japan, Mount Fuji being called Horaizan.

Inspired, Sentaro sought *sennin* for the Water of Eternal Life, journeying through high peaks in vain. Frustrated, he sought guidance at Jofuku-sama's shrine in the southern lands. As he prayed, Jofuku-sama appeared as a luminous spirit. "Sentaro," the spirit resonated, "you are too fond of ease for a *sennin*'s austere path."

"Yet, in answer to your prayer," Jofuku-sama continued, "I shall send you to Tokoyo no Kuni, the Land of Eternity, where death never casts its shadow!" Jofuku-sama placed an *origami tsuru* into Sentaro's hand, instructing him to mount its back. The crane swelled, bearing him aloft, soaring across the ocean for many days. At a verdant island, the *origami tsuru* folded itself and slipped into Sentaro's kimono pocket.

Sentaro settled in Tokoyo no Kuni, where no one ever died. Priests spoke of Gokuraku Jōdo, a Pure Land Paradise attainable only through death. Unlike Sentaro, the islanders yearned for this release, weary of their endless lives. They consumed substances considered poisons, hoping for death, only to find themselves healthier. Sentaro realized he had come to a world turned upside down.

Three hundred years flowed by. Sentaro's life in Tokoyo no Kuni grew less idyllic with failed business and disputes. He grew utterly weary of immortality, yearning for his homeland and mortality's sweet release. An endless, unchanging life was a foolish burden.

Recalling Jofuku-sama, Sentaro prayed to return. The *origami tsuru* fluttered from his pocket, grew, and bore him aloft. A fierce storm arose; the damp crane crumpled, falling into the waves with Sentaro. Terrified, he cried to Jofuku-sama. A monstrous *same*, a shark, bore down. Paralysed, Sentaro screamed for rescue.

Suddenly, Sentaro jolted awake, his own screams echoing. Slumped before Jofuku-sama's shrine, his adventure a vivid, bewildering dream. He was drenched in cold sweat. A luminous messenger appeared, holding a scroll. "I am sent by Jofuku-sama," he spoke. "He permitted you in a dream to experience Tokoyo no Kuni. You grew weary, yearning for death. Jofuku-sama tested you; your desire for death was not true, for you cried out for life."

"It is vain to seek the *sennin*'s path or Elixir. Return to your ancestral home. Live a good, diligent life. Venerate your ancestors, and provide for your children's future. Thus attain venerable age and happiness. Abandon the futile wish to escape death, for no mortal can. You have learned that even granted selfish desires do not bring true contentment." The messenger vanished. Sentaro, taking the lesson to heart, clutched the scroll and returned home. He cast aside his vain wishes, lived a good and useful life, and henceforth, he and his house prospered, living out his natural days with quiet contentment.
